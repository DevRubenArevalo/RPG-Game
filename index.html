A Slime's Journey

Swear fealty to the Slime King, then sprint into the neon mire. Melt foes, hoard boons, and carve your legend from ooze.

How to Play

Move with A/D or arrows, jump with Space/W, and duck with S. Trail acid to melt foes and cash in upgrades every 5,000 distance.

Slime Shop

Choose a boon. Click its tile to infuse your slime.
